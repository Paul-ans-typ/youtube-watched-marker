Where do I get this file?
  1. Go to takeout.google.com.
  2. Deselect all, then tick YouTube and YouTube Music → history.
  3. Export, download and unzip the archive.
  4. Find watch-history.json (or watch-history.html) in Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/history/.
